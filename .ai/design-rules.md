# PersonaKey Hybrid Visual Architecture & Anti-Slop UI Rules

1. **Hybrid Visual Architecture:**
   - **Hardware & Hero Stage (`#071322` / `#0B1E33` Deep Navy Slate):** Used for the Hero and the Interactive Hardware Product Journey to give the physical PersonaKey device and cryptographic beams dramatic hardware-engineering contrast.
   - **Commercial, Problem & B2B Sections (`#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC` Crisp Light Mode):** Used for the Problem Explorer, Neutrality Matrix, Solutions Console, Market Evidence, and Pricing for executive readability.
2. **Color Discipline:**
   - Dark Hardware Stage: Deep Canvas (`#071322`), Elevated Navy (`#0B1E33`), Hairline Slate Borders (`#1E3A5F` / `#334155`), Crisp White Headings (`#FFFFFF`), Muted Slate Copy (`#CBD5E1` / `#94A3B8`), Teal Accent (`#0F8B8D`), and Warm Amber (`#D4A338` — reserved strictly for hardware token & active encryption indicators).
   - Light Commercial Stage: Crisp Slate-White (`#F8FAFC`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Deep Navy Headings (`#0B1E33`), Slate Text (`#334155` / `#64748B`), Hairline Borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), Teal (`#0F8B8D`), and Warm Amber (`#D4A338` — strictly hardware indicators).
   - ZERO purple, indigo, violet, or neon accents anywhere.
3. **Surface & Geometry Discipline:** Use solid flat surfaces only. ZERO `backdrop-blur`, ZERO glassmorphism, ZERO background mesh/radial gradients, ZERO floating 3D blobs, and ZERO decorative drop shadows larger than `shadow-xs`. Cap all card and button border radii at `8px` (`rounded-lg` max; prefer `rounded-md`).
4. **Typography:** Pair `Inter` (clean neo-grotesque) for UI/headings with `JetBrains Mono` for preference schemas, JSON payloads, and status badges. Tight headline tracking (`-0.02em`), high contrast (minimum WCAG AA).
5. **Copy & Layout Discipline:** Write concrete, technical, UK-English copy ("personalisation", "licence", "authorise"). No exclamation-mark hype, no fake customer testimonials, no inflated vanity counters. Every visual block must represent real product mechanics.
